Editing

Draw: click to add points, double-click to finish.

Move: drag a point or waypoint.

Waypoints: click to select, double-click to rename, Delete to remove.

Rotate/Tilt: ⌘/Ctrl + click and drag the map; scroll to zoom.

Delete: select a point and press Delete.

Undo/Redo: use ⌘/Ctrl-Z and ⇧⌘/Ctrl-Z.
